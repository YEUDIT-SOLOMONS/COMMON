Charging shipping fees to a saved card (via Summit)

Default: OFF. Shipping fees are recorded and shown, but nothing is charged.

To turn on, after Summit confirms the terminal supports charging a saved card:
  gcloud run services update photorental-api --region me-west1 --update-env-vars Shipping__ChargeEnabled=true

To turn off again:
  gcloud run services update photorental-api --region me-west1 --update-env-vars Shipping__ChargeEnabled=false

When ON:
- First time a renter has a shipping fee: she pays on Summit's secure page (card is saved), then the order is created.
- Next times: the saved card is charged automatically at order time (the shipment is booked first and cancelled if the charge fails).
- If the order is paid by credit card, the shipping fee is already part of that payment.
- Cancelling a paid courier return sends an email to common@onyxapp.co.il asking for a manual refund.
